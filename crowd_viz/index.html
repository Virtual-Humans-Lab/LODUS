TRANSPARENCY & PROVENANCE

What the map can tell us

These are estimated minimum redistribution flows, not observed trajectories. Equal opposing trips and movement inside a zone are invisible to regional net deltas.

Each particle is a weighted, continuous stream sample. Its speed follows the cached street travel times; samples repeat along routes and are not individual agent identities. Functional allocation changes guide occupation estimates. Age and occupation are assumed independent.

Local/outsider status is location-relative. Flow samples use origin residency; stock layers use the recorded status at each location. Filtering does not identify an observed OD matrix.
